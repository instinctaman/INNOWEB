Overview

This course provides a solid foundation in web development, covering both front-end and back-end technologies. Whether you're a beginner looking to build websites from scratch or an experienced developer aiming to enhance your skills, this course will guide you through essential tools and techniques to create dynamic, responsive, and user-friendly websites.
